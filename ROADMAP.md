# Public product status

This is a status list, not a promise of dates.

## Available in the product now

- Glow as the client for a person's AI, with keys held on the device.
- Network-served answers. The phone is not an offline copy of a large model.
- A flat published price of $0.002 per prompt.
- Personal content kept out of the shared answer store. General answers reusable only after identifying details are removed.
- Consumer devices can help confirm the network. Servers write blocks.
- Kapra settles payments and AI use on the same network.
- KSL is the language used for on-network applications. The language implementation is not part of this public status.

## Measured

- Internal Glow answer-rate runs from 27–30 September 2026, including an hour-long pass at 122.6 answers per second and six-minute passes near 295 answers per second. See the benchmark note. These are our figures.

## Not yet published

- An outside repeat of the benchmark runs.
- Memory, bandwidth, compute, and a comparison against a conventional model API.
- Chain throughput and block finality from a recorded run.
- A measured deployment of 100 devices or 1 million devices. Those sizes are the intended shape only.

## Not claimed

- That every phone writes blocks.
- That Glow runs a full foundation model offline.
- That the published papers are enough to rebuild the system.
